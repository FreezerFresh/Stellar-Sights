These files are for the light curves.
Note:
Use the time column for the x-axis and the value column for the y-axis.
Some planets do not have light-curve data available from NASA. For those planets, the CSV contains a note in the notes column explaining that no data was provided on the website.

relative_flux - Brightness compared to the normal level of the star (1.0 = normal).

normalized_flux - Same idea as relative_flux. Brightness scaled so the baseline is about 1.0.

magnitude - How bright the star looks, measured in magnitudes. Higher number = dimmer star.

differential_magnitude - Difference in magnitude compared to a reference. Positive or negative numbers show brighter or dimmer than the reference.
